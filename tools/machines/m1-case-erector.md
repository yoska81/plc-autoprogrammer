# Case erector

We need a machine that takes flat cardboard boxes from a stack and opens them up into boxes, folds the bottom flaps and tapes the bottom. Right now two people do this by hand and it's killing their backs.

The flat boxes sit in a magazine (a rack, maybe 100 boxes). A suction cup arm grabs the front box and pulls it open — we think it's an air cylinder with suction cups and a vacuum generator. Then little air cylinders fold the small flaps, and the box gets pushed through a tape head that tapes the bottom (the tape head is just mechanical, no power). Then it goes out on a short powered roller conveyor.

We want about 10 boxes a minute. We need to know when the magazine is almost empty (a sensor), when the box didn't open (vacuum switch?), and if a box jams at the tape head. There are doors around it with switches — if someone opens a door everything must stop. E-stop on the panel. Plant has 480 V 3-phase and air. We'd like a touch screen to see counts and faults, and to be able to cycle each cylinder by hand when setting it up.
