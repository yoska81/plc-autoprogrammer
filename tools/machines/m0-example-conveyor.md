# Conveyor cell (the app's example; replay uses the E2E mock answers, not real AI)

Conveyor cell that moves parts to an end station where a pneumatic pusher pushes each part off onto a take-away table. About 10 parts per minute. Start and Stop buttons, an E-stop into a safety relay, a photo-eye at the end and a prox that sees the pusher out. Count the parts. 480 V, 1 HP conveyor motor.
