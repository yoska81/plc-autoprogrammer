# Pick and place with servo

Parts come down a belt conveyor one at a time. At the end there's a sensor. We want a gantry that picks the part (vacuum cup on a small up/down air cylinder) and moves it sideways about 600 mm to put it in a tray. The sideways move should be a servo motor so it's fast and accurate — we'd like two drop positions (tray A and tray B) chosen from the screen. The belt is a small 1/2 HP motor, 230 V 3-phase, it just runs forward.

We want maybe 30 picks a minute. The servo must home when the machine starts. If the vacuum doesn't make, try once more then alarm. There's a light curtain on the front where the operator swaps trays — breaking it should stop the gantry safely. E-stop too. Power is 230 V 3-phase. Touch screen to pick the tray, see counts, jog the servo for setup.
