# Mixing tank

We have a 500 gallon stainless tank where we mix a product. We add water (one valve), then two ingredients from day tanks (a valve and a pump each), mix it with an agitator (5 HP motor) and heat it with a steam jacket (steam valve that can open partly). We need to see the level (there's a level transmitter, 4-20 mA) and the temperature (RTD or 4-20 mA transmitter) on the screen.

Recipe: fill water to a level, add ingredient A until a level, add ingredient B until a level, mix for X minutes while heating to Y degrees, hold at temperature for Z minutes, then open the drain valve and run the transfer pump until the tank is empty (low level switch). We want to type X, Y, Z and the levels on a screen and keep a few recipes. Alarms: high level switch (overflow), temperature too high, agitator overload, pump overloads. Agitator only runs if the lid is closed (switch). 480 V 3-phase. Motors: agitator 5 HP, transfer pump 3 HP, ingredient pumps 1 HP each.
