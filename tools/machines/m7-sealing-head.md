# Sealing head

Sealing head: a pneumatic heat-sealing head closes on a pouch, holds for the dwell time at the set temperature, then opens. Two-hand start, light curtain, heater with thermocouple. The film comes off an unwinder driven by a 0.5 HP motor on a VFD. CLICK PLC with a C-more 7" touch panel; the operator sets the seal temperature and the dwell time on the HMI. 480 V 3-phase supply.
